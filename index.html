About

I am a Graduate Research Assistant at the Centre for Urban Informatics, Technology and Policy (CITY) at LUMS, advised by Muhammad Tahir and Zubair Khalid. I am also a Teaching Fellow, MSAI.

My research is in AI safety and mechanistic interpretability. I currently study emergent misalignment: how narrow fine-tuning produces broadly misaligned behaviour, and which circuits and features inside the model are responsible. I want these behaviours to be measurable and controllable at the level of model internals, not only at the level of outputs.

Previously, I used circuit-level analysis to show that FedAvg under non-IID data preserves client representations but misaligns them with the global classifier (ICML 2026 MI Workshop). I have also worked on model compression (BaCP, KLAWQ) and domain generalization. In summer 2025 I was a research intern at UIUC with Darko Marinov and Reyhaneh Jabbarvand on LLMs for software engineering.
