I am a Graduate Research Assistant at the Centre for Urban Informatics, Technology and Policy (CITY) at LUMS, working with Muhammad Tahir and Zubair Khalid, and a Graduate Teaching Fellow for the MS in Artificial Intelligence program, where I teach AI 630: Responsible AI Engineering. I completed my B.S. in Computer Science at LUMS in 2026.

I work on AI safety through mechanistic interpretability. My current focus is emergent misalignment: how narrow fine-tuning produces broadly misaligned behaviour, and which circuits and features inside the model carry it. I want these behaviours to be measurable and controllable at the level of model internals, not only at the level of outputs.

Before this I studied federated learning with the same tools, showing that FedAvg under non-IID data preserves client representations but misaligns them with the global classifier (ICML 2026 MI Workshop). I have also worked on model compression (BaCP, KLAWQ) and domain generalization, and spent summer 2025 at UIUC with Darko Marinov and Reyhaneh Jabbarvand on LLMs for software engineering.
