I am a Graduate Research Assistant at the Centre for Urban Informatics, Technology and Policy (CITY) at LUMS, where I work with Dr. Muhammad Tahir and Dr. Zubair Khalid. I am also a Graduate Teaching Fellow for the MS in Artificial Intelligence program, where I teach AI 630: Responsible AI Engineering. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from LUMS in 2026.

My research is in AI safety and mechanistic interpretability. I currently study emergent misalignment: how narrow fine-tuning produces broadly misaligned behaviour, and which internal circuits and features are responsible for it. My goal is to make these behaviours measurable and controllable at the level of model internals rather than only at the level of outputs.

Previously, I used circuit-level analysis to show that FedAvg under non-IID data preserves client representations but misaligns them with the global classifier (ICML 2026 Mechanistic Interpretability Workshop), and worked on model compression (BaCP, KLAWQ) and domain generalization. In summer 2025 I was a research intern at UIUC with Darko Marinov and Reyhaneh Jabbarvand, working on LLMs for software engineering.

I am happy to talk about research or collaborations — 26100253@lums.edu.pk.
