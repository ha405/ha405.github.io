About

My research focuses on AI safety and mechanistic interpretability. I am currently studying emergent misalignment after narrow fine-tuning: the circuits associated with this behaviour, whether targeted interventions can detect or reduce it, and how well those interventions transfer across models and tasks.

From January 2025 to May 2026, I was a Research Assistant at the Centre for Urban Informatics, Technology and Policy (CITY) at LUMS, working with Muhammad Tahir and Zubair Khalid. I studied representation and classifier alignment in FedAvg under non-IID data (ICML 2026 MI Workshop). Other projects include model compression (BaCP and KLAWQ) and domain generalization.

During a 2025 research internship at UIUC, I worked with Darko Marinov and Reyhaneh Jabbarvand on applications of LLMs to software engineering.
